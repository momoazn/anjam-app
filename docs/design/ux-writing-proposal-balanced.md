# Anjam App UX Writing Proposal - Balanced Edition

## Professional yet Approachable Persian Interface Text

### Executive Summary

This proposal presents a balanced approach to Persian UX writing for Anjam, maintaining professional credibility while creating a warm, approachable user experience. The tone is calibrated for business users who appreciate efficiency with a human touch.

---

## Tone Guidelines

### The Sweet Spot

- **Professional**: Clear, respectful, business-appropriate
- **Warm**: Approachable, encouraging, human-centered
- **Efficient**: Concise, action-focused, time-conscious
- **Cultural**: Natural Persian flow, familiar references

### What We Avoid

- ❌ Overly casual: `بسازش!` / `چی می‌خوای انجام بدی؟`
- ❌ Overly formal: `لطفاً اطلاعات ورود خویش را وارد فرمایید`
- ✅ Just right: `تسک جدید بسازید` / `چه کاری باید انجام شود؟`

## 8. Professional Polish

### Enhanced Clarity

- **Time References:** Use precise business language
  - `ساعت` instead of informal time references
  - `تاریخ تحویل` instead of just `مهلت`

- **Action Specificity:** Be explicit about actions
  - `ایجاد تسک جدید` instead of `تسک جدید`
  - `حذف این تسک` instead of just `حذف`

- **Status Clarity:** Use business-appropriate terminology
  - `در دست اجرا` instead of `در حال انجام`
  - `تکمیل شده` instead of `انجام شده`

### Cultural Professionalism

- Maintain formal business Persian structure
- Use appropriate honorifics in user communications
- Include proper business calendar references
- Ensure technical accuracy with natural flow

## 12. Conclusion

This balanced approach positions Anjam as a professional-grade project management tool that respects its users while remaining approachable. The tone strikes the perfect balance between corporate credibility and user-friendly communication, making it suitable for:

- Enterprise teams requiring professional interfaces
- Business users who value efficiency and clarity
- Organizations needing credible project management tools
- Users who appreciate respectful, clear communication

The proposed UX writing enhances user confidence, reduces cognitive load, and maintains the professional standards expected in business environments while feeling naturally Persian and culturally appropriate.
